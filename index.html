Seed c

Extended precision pairs two float32s to reach ~1e-15 accuracy, taking the usable zoom from about 10⁴ to 10¹². Auto engages it as soon as a pixel gets close to the float32 step size, which is what otherwise turns a deep zoom into square blocks. It covers +, −, ×, ÷ and integer powers only — formulas using sin, exp and friends stay single precision, and the badge in the corner turns amber once they run out of detail.

Custom formulas

Enter the right-hand side of zn+1 = f(z, c). z is zn.

  • Operators + − * / ** ^, implicit products (2z)
  • Names z c n i pi tau e phi
  • Functions abs conj re im len norm sqrt exp log sin cos tan sinh cosh tanh pow
  • abs(z) folds each component (Burning Ship), len(z) is the modulus

Holomorphic formulas get a symbolically differentiated orbit derivative, which drives distance-field colouring and relief shading. Formulas built only from + − × ÷ and integer powers also get an extended-precision version for deep zooms.

If a formula leaves almost nothing escaping — rational maps such as (z**2 + c)/(z**2 - c) — switch the colour mode to Stripe average or Orbit trap. Those read the whole orbit, so they still show structure where escape time has nothing to say.
